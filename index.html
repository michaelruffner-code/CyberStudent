How Legacy Student Videos and Neptune Navigate fit together

Each works well on its own. Legacy Student Videos are quick and flexible, ideal for a short lesson or class discussion. Neptune Navigate is a structured program organized by grade band with assessments. Some districts use both, using the videos to reinforce Neptune topics. Your Customer Success Manager can help you choose.

Assign, lead, or mix

Student Phishing

Simulations, reporting practice, and reinforcement of safe behaviors, managed in the CyberNut Admin Portal.

Realistic

Student-Specific Senders

Templates based on the school tools, games, and brands students use. Simulations get harder as each student improves.

Reinforcing

Microlessons and Acorns

Every click gets instant feedback, and short microlessons teach what to look for. Students earn acorns for spotting scams and climb class leaderboards.

Practice

Reporting Practice

Students practice recognizing and reporting suspicious emails through simulations. CyberNut can also help districts identify and remediate real email threats, depending on enabled services. If a reported phish comes from a district account, CyberNut flags it as likely compromised and alerts admins, and districts can turn on auto-suspend.

Step 4 · Measure Success

Measure Success

Check in on three things throughout the year.

Lesson Completion

Are students finishing lessons?

In Neptune Navigate, use the gradebook, Student Progress Report, Navigate District Report, and Pre-K / K Summary Report. Export on demand, on a schedule, or from a past month.

Student Engagement

Are students participating?

Legacy Student Videos participation is teacher-observed through class discussion and printable quizzes. Neptune Navigate (quiz results) and Student Phishing (acorns and leaderboards) offer platform-based metrics.

Reporting Behavior

Are students reporting phishing?

Review campaign results by student, school, and district in the CyberNut Admin Portal to see reporting grow over time.

If engagement or reporting is low, resend the teacher and student templates, pair a related lesson with a friendly competition, and contact your Customer Success Manager for help.

Completion records can be exported and may help document your district's compliance efforts.
