How Legacy Videos and Neptune Navigate fit together

Each works well on its own. Legacy videos are quick and flexible, ideal for a short lesson or class discussion. Neptune Navigate is a structured, grade by grade program with assessments. Some districts use both, using the videos to reinforce Neptune topics. Your Customer Success Manager can help you choose.

Continue the Conversation for Parents

EN What is it?

Continue the Conversation helps families keep learning at home. Each of the eight Neptune Navigate lessons has a short guide for parents, so they know what their child learned and can talk about it together. Share the link in your newsletter, on your website, or after each lesson.

ES ¿Qué es?

Continuar la Conversación ayuda a las familias a seguir aprendiendo en casa. Cada una de las ocho lecciones de Neptune Navigate tiene una guía breve para padres, para que sepan lo que su hijo aprendió y puedan hablar del tema juntos. Comparta el enlace en su boletín, en su sitio web o después de cada lección.

Offering 3

Student Phishing

Simulations, reporting practice, and reinforcement of safe behaviors, managed in the CyberNut admin portal.

Realistic

Student Specific Senders

Templates based on the school tools, games, and brands students use. Simulations get harder as each student improves.

Reinforcing

Microlessons and Points

Short microlessons teach what to look for. Students earn points for spotting scams and climb class leaderboards.

Practice

Reporting and Removal

Students practice flagging suspicious messages, and reported threats can be removed from student inboxes. CyberAutomations can score and remove reported threats and catch compromised accounts automatically.

Step 4 · Measure Success

Measure Success

Check in on three things throughout the year.

Lesson Completion

Are students finishing lessons?

In Neptune Navigate, use the gradebook, Student Progress Report, Navigate District Report, and Pre-K / K Summary Report.

Student Engagement

Are students participating?

Watch Neptune quiz results, class discussion after Legacy Videos, and points and leaderboards in Student Phishing.

Reporting Behavior

Are students reporting phishing?

Review campaign results by student, school, and district in the admin portal to see reporting grow over time.

Completion records can be exported and may help document your district's compliance efforts.
